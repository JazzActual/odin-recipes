BBQ Chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds bone-in skin-on chicken thighs
  • Your favorite dry barbecue seasoning or rub
  • ~1 cup of your favorite barbecue sauce
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 F
  2. Using paper towels: dry off the chicken thighs
  3. Season with your dry rub and salt. Be sure to season all sides of the chicken!
  4. Place the chicken on a baking sheet in the oven for 20 minutes
  5. After 20 minutes: baste chicken with your barbecue sauce
  6. Place chicken back into the oven for approximately 15 minutes or 165 F internal
  7. Rest chicken for 10 minutes and enjoy!
