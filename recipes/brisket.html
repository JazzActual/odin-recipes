Brisket

Ingredients

  • Brisket of your choosing
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
  • Garlic Powder
  • Yellow Mustard

Steps

  1. Set your pellet grill to 225 F
  2. Mix together your salt, pepper, and garlic at a ratio of 2 parts black pepper, 1 part salt, and half a part garlic powder
  3. Trim off excess fat to allow an approximately half inch layer of fat
  4. Thoroughly coat every surface with the mustard
  5. Apply your salt, pepper, garlic blend thoroughly to every part of the exterior of the brisket
  6. Place in preheated smoker until an internal temperature of 195 F
  7. Remove brisket from smoker and wrap with butcher paper. Wrap the butcher paper package with old towels and place in cooler to rest for 2 hours
  8. Enjoy!
